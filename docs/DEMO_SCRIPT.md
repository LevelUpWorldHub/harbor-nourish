# Demo script — 3 to 5 minutes

Use this for the Phase 1 video. Narration is welcome. Record the phone-width browser, or a phone, with the synthetic demo loaded. Speak slowly. Large type is already on.

On-screen title card, about 5 seconds:

**Harbor**  
Nutrition and flare-watch companion  
Level Up Inc. · Mark Yukhimets  
Synthetic demo. Not a real person. Not medical advice.

Total target: about 4 minutes 30 seconds.

## 0:00–0:25 — What it is, and what it refuses

Open the first screen before the journal loads (or use a fresh browser profile).

Say:

> Harbor helps someone living with an autoimmune condition keep meals and flares in one place. It does not tell them what illness they have, what to eat, or what medicine to take. The journal stays on the device.

Point at the three sentences. Tap **Load the synthetic demo**.

> Avery Chen is a labeled synthetic persona, so we are not showing anyone’s real health data.

## 0:25–1:20 — Gentle check-in, with the person’s own logs

Land on Today. The demo clock is 24 October 2026, 3:00 PM. Point at that label so reviewers see it is not “today” on the recording device.

Read the gentle check-in. Emphasize “not a prediction.”

Tap one citation (a meal and, if you have time, a flare mark). Show the log detail.

Say:

> The warning explains itself and points at Avery’s own meals, sleep, and check-ins. Harbor does not fetch a generic diet rule from the internet.

Tap **Fatigue day** and show the larger type and the shorter Today page. Turn it off again if the rest of the video needs the extra links.

## 1:20–2:05 — Patterns

Open **Patterns**. Open one food pattern and one sleep pattern. Open **How this was counted**.

Say:

> It waits for at least two flares the person marked. It compares the 48 hours before those flares with their other days. The simulated watch numbers are labeled. This is a pattern in one journal, not a cause, and not a study result.

## 2:05–2:40 — Journal

Open **Journal**. Show the 0-to-10 marks and the flare button.

Say:

> The person decides what a flare is. On a fatigue day, only pain, fatigue, and the flare mark are required. The rest can wait.

You do not need to save a new entry unless you want to show the saved line.

## 2:40–3:25 — Meal, confirmed by the person

Open **Meals**, then **Log a meal**. Tap the sample plate **Boxed macaroni and salted crackers**. Say it is a drawing, not the user’s food.

Leave photo consent off. Tap **Match tags on this device**. On the confirm screen, remove one tag and tap **This looks right — save**.

Say:

> Tags are suggestions. Nothing is stored until the person confirms them. With an xAI key and consent, the same screen can ask Grok to read a real photo. Without a key, this on-device match is the fallback. Symptoms are never part of that request.

If a key is configured and you want to show it, use a photo of food, not a face, and say consent is on. Do not show the API key.

## 3:25–3:45 — Simulated signals

Open **Signals** from Today or by going to `/signals`. Read the banner aloud.

> This feed is simulated. It is not a watch. It shows where sleep, steps, and resting heart rate would sit later.

## 3:45–4:15 — Visit summary

Open **Visit**. Scroll the flare list and one pattern with citations. Tap **Print or save as PDF** and show the print preview, then cancel. Mention the HTML download.

> This is a sheet to take to an appointment. It repeats that Harbor is not a diagnosis or a treatment plan.

## 4:15–4:40 — Privacy and self-healing

Open **Privacy**. Show consent off, and the list of what can leave the device.

Open **Status**. Tap **Run checks**. Then tap **Practice a recovered failure** or **Practice a full outage**.

> If Grok is slow or missing, Harbor retries once and then matches tags on the device. The journal still opens.

## 4:40–5:00 — Close

Return to Today.

> Harbor’s job is to remember, to show the person’s own pattern with the logs attached, and to leave the decision with them and with their clinician. Phase 2 would put this same app on iOS and Android, and would test it with people who live with these conditions. That testing is still ahead of us.

End card:

Discuss what you notice with your clinician.  
Synthetic demo. AI use disclosed in the submission.
